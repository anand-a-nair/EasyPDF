# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Critique work, not persons.

## Expected behavior

- Treat everyone with respect, regardless of experience, background, or identity
- Give feedback on the substance of the contribution, not the contributor
- Accept that others may disagree, and that disagreement about technical
  direction is normal and healthy
- Assume good intent when reading terse or imperfect English — most
  contributors are not writing in their first language

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others'
private information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Technical disagreement

Strong technical opinions are welcome — this project has several, written down
in [`ideas/`](ideas/). Argue the merits, bring evidence, and accept the outcome
once a decision is recorded. "I think this is wrong because X" is always
welcome. Repeatedly relitigating a settled decision without new information is
not.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer at the email on the repository owner's
profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Consequences range from a warning
to a permanent ban, depending on severity.

## Attribution

Inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
deliberately shortened — a code of conduct nobody reads protects nobody.
